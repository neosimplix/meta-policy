Creator Discovery

Neosimplix internal marketing tool Not signed in
Step 1 — Sign in

Connect your Facebook account

Sign in to grant this tool access to the Facebook Page and Instagram professional account your business owns.

This button opens Facebook Login for Business. On the consent screen the brand administrator grants “Discover content creators on the Instagram Creator Marketplace platform” (instagram_creator_marketplace_discovery) and the Page / Instagram permissions the search depends on.

Step 2 — Select Page

Choose the Facebook Page for your brand

Uses pages_show_list to list the Pages you manage, then instagram_basic to resolve the connected Instagram professional account. The Page access token returned here is the token every Creator Marketplace request in Step 3 is sent with.

Step 3 — Find creators

Search Instagram Creator Marketplace

Uses instagram_creator_marketplace_discovery to search creators and read their profile and audience insights.

Pick the creator country and interest, then search. Each result shows the creator’s bio and follower count. Click View insights to see one creator’s followers, engaged accounts and account reach.
