Privacy Policy

네오심플릭스 · Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 네오심플릭스 ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and deletes information obtained through our internal influencer discovery application ("the App"), which accesses the Meta Platform via the Instagram Creator Marketplace API and related Meta APIs.

The App is an internal business tool. It is not offered to the general public and does not provide consumer accounts.

1. Information we collect

Through the Meta Platform, with permissions granted by an authorized administrator of our own business assets, the App may receive:

CategoryExamples
Creator profile dataInstagram username, biography, profile picture URL, country, gender, age bucket, verification status, marketplace onboarding status
Creator performance dataTotal followers, engaged accounts, reach, views, interactions, engagement rate
Audience demographicsAggregated follower distribution by age, gender, and country
Media metadataPost captions, permalinks, thumbnails, like and comment counts, tagged brands
Our own business dataFacebook Page identifiers, connected Instagram business account identifiers, marketplace status of our own brand

We do not collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or contact details of Instagram users. We do not collect data from individuals who are not Instagram professional accounts participating in Meta's Creator Marketplace.

2. How we use it

  • Identifying creators whose audience matches our brand's target profile
  • Evaluating creators before proposing a paid partnership
  • Internal reporting on campaign planning and creator selection

We do not sell, rent, or license this data. We do not use it for advertising targeting outside the Meta Platform, and we do not combine it with data obtained from unauthorized sources.

3. Who can access it

Access is limited to employees and contractors of 네오심플릭스 who require it for the purposes above. Data is stored on access-controlled infrastructure. We do not share this data with third parties except service providers that process it on our behalf under contract (for example, cloud hosting), and only as required to operate the App.

4. Retention and deletion

We retain creator data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, and no longer than 24 months from collection, after which it is deleted.

Data is deleted sooner when:

  • A creator or Instagram user submits a deletion request (see Data Deletion)
  • A creator leaves Meta's Creator Marketplace or withdraws marketplace consent
  • Our access to the Meta Platform is revoked or the App is decommissioned

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you. Requests are handled within 30 days. See Data Deletion for the procedure.

6. Compliance

Our handling of Meta Platform data follows the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Personal information is processed in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
